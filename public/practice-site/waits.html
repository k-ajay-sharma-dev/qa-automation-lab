Waits & synchronisation

Everything here is slow on purpose. If your test passes only with Thread.sleep, it is not done yet.

1. Banner injected after page load

About 4 seconds after the page loads, a promo banner is added to the DOM (it does not exist before). Practise presenceOfElementLocated.

2. Hidden message becomes visible

The message is already in the DOM but hidden. 2 seconds after the click it becomes visible. Practise visibilityOfElementLocated.

Your coupon code is WAIT4IT

3. Loading spinner

Click load. A spinner is shown for ~3 seconds, then disappears and the data appears. Practise invisibilityOfElementLocated.

4. Button enabled later

"Claim reward" is disabled for 5 seconds after page load. Practise elementToBeClickable.

5. Status text that changes

After clicking "Place order" the status goes Pending → Processing → Completed over ~4 seconds. Practise textToBePresentInElementLocated.

Order status: Not started

6. Re-rendered list (StaleElementReferenceException)

"Refresh prices" throws away the whole list and builds a new one (like a React re-render). Any WebElement you found before the click is now stale.

7. "Load more" (AJAX-like)

Each click fetches 5 more reviews after ~2 seconds. 15 reviews in total, then the button disappears. Practise waiting for the item count to grow.

No more reviews.
